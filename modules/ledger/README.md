# ledger

**Responsibility:** accounts and transactions, and the classification of each movement. It is the source of truth about what happened to the money.

**Not its responsibility:** talking to Pluggy (that is `ingest`) or choosing categories (that is `categorize`). The `ledger` stores the category it receives and where it came from.

## Concepts

- `Account` (aggregate, `account.go`): checking account, savings account or credit card. Its balance is from my point of view: negative on a card (the amount owed on the open bill). Only the last four digits of its number are kept. A card also keeps `CardDetails`: its limit and what is still available, and the bill's due and closing days, each one only when the bank reports it.
- `Transaction` (aggregate, `transaction.go`): one movement in an account. The amount is negative when money leaves the account, on checking and on cards alike. Its kind is the source's until the transaction is paired; while paired, the pair's. An expense or income I mark as a transfer (money to an account lastro does not see, which the matcher can never pair) counts as a transfer until I unmark it; the mark survives every sync, and a paired transaction cannot take it.
- `BalanceSnapshot` (in `account.go`): an account's balance on a day, the last one known that day. Every `SyncAccount` records one, so the net worth history (M4) starts with the first sync.
- `Pair` (in `transaction.go`): links the two sides of a movement between my own accounts. Into a card it makes both sides a card bill payment; between bank accounts, a transfer.
- Value objects (`values.go`): `ExternalID` (the source's id, today Pluggy's), `Kind` (expense, income, transfer, card bill payment; only the first two count in totals), `Installment` ("3 of 10"), `CategoryID` (opaque, owned by `categorize`), `Source` (rule, LLM or manual), `Status` (pending or posted), `AccountType` (checking, savings, credit card) and `CardDetails`. Constructors return an error wrapping `ErrInvalid` instead of an invalid value.
- `BalanceHistory` (`history.go`): rebuilds an account's balance at the end of each day, walking back from the bank's latest balance through each day's net. Days after that balance are left out: the bank does not count them yet.
- `GroupInstallments` (`installments.go`): the source has no purchase id, so a `Purchase` is the installments with the same description (without the "3/12" Nubank appends, ignoring case), the same count and the same amount, within a cent per installment (the rounding lands on the first one). A number appears once per purchase. A purchase knows its current installment, what remains and its last day, a month apart per installment the source has not sent.
- A transaction I add by hand to a synced account (checking, savings or a card) is one the source failed to send, such as a Pix Meu Pluggy never collected. Its external id starts with `manual-`. The bank's balance already counts it, so it moves no balance, but it fills in the balance history and the month's totals.
- `PossibleDuplicate` and `Merge` (`duplicates.go`, domain services): when the source later sends a transaction in the same account with the same amount, up to 3 days from one I added, the two may be one movement told twice. The ledger never decides on its own, because two Pix of the same amount in a week are common: it asks. The same: `Merge` keeps the synced one, which takes my category (unless I chose its own), my transfer mark, my note when it has none, my tags and my split when it has none, and removes mine. Two: the pair is kept apart (`ledger.kept_apart`) and never suggested again, nor merged.
- `CardBills` (`bills.go`, a domain service): a card's bills from its closed bills (`Bill`, as the source reports them: due date, total, minimum, interest and fees, stored in `ledger.bills` by `ImportBills`) and its live transactions, each with the source's bill reference (`bill_ref`, opaque). A transaction goes into the closed bill its reference names; without any closed bill stored, posted transactions sharing a reference make one, without a due date. The rest: dated after today, the installments to come (`next`); pending, or posted after the last closed bill (one I added), the open bill, due a month after the last closed one (or on the card's due day). A closed bill's total is the source's; what it holds beyond its own transactions and charges is what the bill before left unpaid. A bill payment pays the closed bill before the one it is credited in, or the latest closed one while it is on the open bill; what that bill no longer owes was paid ahead on the open bill (once it closes, the source's total already has it): `paid`, `partly_paid`, `unpaid` (not due yet) or `overdue`. Each bill has its purchases, installments and refunds, what it spent by category (a split by its parts) and its transactions.
- `TransferMatcher` (`MatchTransfers` in `transfer.go`, a domain service): among unpaired transactions, pairs an outflow from one of my bank accounts with an inflow of the same amount in another account. Into a card, the inflow must be what the card calls a bill payment, up to 3 days apart; between bank accounts, an expense with an income, up to 1 day apart. Closer dates pair first, and an outflow or inflow with two equally good candidates in different accounts is left alone rather than guessed. `ingest` runs it after every sync.

## Invariants

- A transaction is unique per account and `ExternalID`. Importing again never duplicates: it is an upsert. When the source recreates a transaction with a new `ExternalID` but the same bank reference, the existing one is re-keyed, keeping its category.
- An account's type and currency never change, and neither does a transaction's currency: it is always the account's.
- Card bill payments and transfers between my own accounts never count as expense or income.
- A pair joins an outflow and an inflow of the same amount in two different accounts, never out of a card. If one side is removed or its amount changes, the pair breaks on both sides and each gets its source's kind back.
- A manual category beats an automatic one. Rules and the LLM never overwrite a manual choice (`ErrManualCategory`).
- Pending transactions may change or disappear on the next sync. A transaction the source stops returning is removed (kept, but out of every list and total); if it comes back, it is restored. One I added by hand is never removed by a sync: the source never sends it. Removed transactions cannot be categorized or paired. Only posted transactions go into closed reports.
- Only a credit card has card details, in the account's currency, with a limit that is never negative (what is available can be, over the limit). They travel with the balance: an older read replaces neither.
- What I add to a transaction (a note up to 500 characters, up to 10 tags, a split) is never touched by a sync. A tag is lowercase letters and digits joined by dashes.
- A split divides an expense or income across 2 to 10 different categories, every part on the transaction's side, adding up to its amount exactly. The largest part becomes the category, chosen by me. Totals by category (`category_lines`) count the parts; a split the bank's restatement no longer adds up is ignored until it is split again. Choosing one category by hand removes the split.
- Accounts outside Open Finance are typed in, under "Outside Open Finance": a wallet (`cash`) takes transactions I add, which go through the rules like synced ones and move its balance; the TransferMatcher pairs a withdrawal into it, never its spending. An asset (`asset`) only takes balances I count, one per day (a correction replaces the day's) and the latest day's is its balance; its history is those balances. Their days turn in São Paulo. Only an asset takes a balance by hand. A wallet and a synced account take transactions by hand, and only one I added can be removed (in a wallet its amount goes back). A split transaction takes one category only after its split is removed.
- An undo puts back a category exactly as it was (`Restore`), a manual one or none included.
- A balance older than the one already known never replaces it, in the account or in its day's snapshot. There is at most one snapshot per account per day.

## Read side

`views.go` holds the DTOs the screens read, straight from SQL (CQRS-lite), and `http.go` serves them:

- `GET /api/v1/accounts`: every account with its latest balance and, for a card, `card` (limit, available, due and closing days, each null when unknown).
- `GET /api/v1/accounts/{id}`: one account, the same shape.
- `GET /api/v1/accounts/{id}/balance`: the balance at the end of each day (`BalanceHistory`).
- `GET /api/v1/accounts/{id}/months`: each month in and out, what counts (spending, refunds), and its flows by kind, direction and, for pairs, the other side's account. Months after today's are `future`: on a card, the installments already committed.
- `GET /api/v1/accounts/{id}/installments`: the purchases with installments still to come, the one ending first first.
- `GET /api/v1/accounts/{id}/bills`: a card's bills (`CardBills`), the next ones, the open one, then the closed ones, the latest first: `id` (the source's, or `open` and `next`), `state`, due date, last transaction's day, total, minimum, interest and fees, what the bill before left in it, purchases, installments and refunds (each amount and count), what was paid, when and from which account, `byCategory` and `transactions`. 400 for an account that is not a card.
- `GET /api/v1/transactions?month=2026-10&day=2026-10-22&account=<id>&flow=in|out|transfer&category=<id>|none&q=<text>`: a month (or one of its days) of live transactions, newest first, each with its account and, for pairs, the other side and its account, plus a summary (count, pending, money in and out that counts). The search ignores case and accents and reads notes too; `tag=` keeps one tag; `scope=all` searches every month (only with `q` or `tag`). Each row has `note`, `tags` and `splitCategories` (largest part first). `category=none` lists the expenses and income still waiting for a category.
- `PUT /api/v1/transactions/transfer` `{"ids", "transfer"}`: marks (or unmarks) up to 5000 transactions as transfers between my own accounts; answers `{"changed"}`. Each row has `markedTransfer`.
- `PUT /api/v1/transactions/{id}/note` `{"note"}`, `PUT /api/v1/transactions/{id}/tags` `{"tags"}` and `DELETE /api/v1/transactions/{id}/split`: 204. The split itself is set through `categorize` (`PUT /api/v1/transactions/{id}/split`), which checks the categories are live.
- `POST /api/v1/accounts` `{"name", "type": "cash" | "asset", "balanceCents"}`, `PUT /api/v1/accounts/{id}/balance` `{"date", "balanceCents"}`, `DELETE /api/v1/accounts/{id}/balance/{date}`, `POST /api/v1/accounts/{id}/transactions` `{"date", "description", "amountCents"}` (negative for money out; a wallet entry, or one a synced account's source failed to send) and `DELETE /api/v1/transactions/{id}` (only one I added). Accounts and transactions carry `manual`.
- `GET /api/v1/transactions/duplicates`: each transaction I added that may be a synced one told twice, with it, closest dates first. `POST /api/v1/transactions/{id}/duplicate` `{"of": "<synced id>", "same": true|false}`: the same merges them into the synced one, not keeps both and stops asking; 409 when the two cannot be one movement (or were kept apart). `GET /api/v1/transactions/{id}` carries `duplicates`, the transactions on the other side. `app` lists each pair among the alerts (`possible_duplicate`).
- `GET /api/v1/tags`: every tag in use with its count, money out and in, and last date; `GET /api/v1/tags/{tag}`: that tag's money by category over every month.
- `GET /api/v1/transactions/{id}`: one transaction, its `split`, the other side of its pair, its purchase's installments and how often its description came up in the last six months.
- `GET /api/v1/transactions/days?month=2026-10`: spending, money in and money moved day by day, every account together, with the month before and what each had come to by the same day. It takes the list's filters (`account`, `category`, `flow`, `q`, `tag`, a split counted by its part), the month before under the same ones; it is always the whole month (`day` is ignored, `scope=all` refused).

For `ingest`, besides the accounts and transactions, `ImportBills(account, bills)` stores a card's closed bills as the source reports them now (a bill read again replaces what it said); each imported transaction carries its bill reference.

For `categorize`, the Service answers what automation may categorize: live expenses and income whose category nobody chose by hand. `Categorizable(ids)` filters the given transactions; `Uncategorized()` returns every one still without a category; `Uncategorize(id)` takes back a category automation gave (never a manual one). Transfers and card bill payments are never handed over.

For `budget`, the Service sums what the budget is built on: `CategoryMonths(from, to)` (expenses and income by month, category and kind), `MonthKinds(from, to)` (every transaction by month, kind, direction and pairing: income, and transfers out to accounts outside lastro) and `Charges(from)` (expenses outside installments, where recurring charges are found).

## Events

- Publishes: `TransactionsImported` (ids of new or changed transactions, after the import commits), `TransactionCategorized` (recorded by the aggregate, published after the commit).
- Consumes: none. It receives commands from `ingest` and `categorize` through its Service.
